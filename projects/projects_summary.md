### PhD Thesis Research — Metasurface-Enhanced Nonlinear Optics
- **Challenge** Enable efficient solid-state photon frequency conversion by engineering strong light–matter interactions in low-loss quantum materials.
- **Technical Strategy**
  + Designed infrared metasurface resonators using FDTD and FEM simulations (CST / COMSOL) to maximize near-field enhancement and mode overlap.
  + Achieved ~10× local plasmonic field enhancement at target IR frequency through geometry optimization and dispersion engineering.
  + Partnered with materials scientists to integrate epitaxially grown quantum materials into nanofabricated metasurface platforms.
  + Developed a nonlinear optical measurement system (high-power IR excitation, cryogenic platform, lock-in detection, data acquisition pipelines in Python/MATLAB).
- **Impact**
+ Demonstrated ~100× enhancement in second-harmonic generation (SHG) relative to unpatterned films.
+ Established quantitative agreement between EM simulations and experimental nonlinear response.
+ Reduced experimental design iteration cycles via predictive modeling.
- **Role & Ownership**
+ Led device design from concept → simulation → fabrication coordination → experimental validation.
+ Owned electromagnetic modeling, field enhancement optimization, and nonlinear characterization workflow.
