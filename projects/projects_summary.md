### PhD Thesis Research
- **Problem:** Photon frequency conversion applications require efficient photon-photon interactions in a solid-state medium. Quantum materials offer a low-loss medium for nonlinear photon interactions. Combining quantum materials with metasurface design principles allows conversion efficiency to be maximized. 
- **Approach:** I used FDTD/FEM to design a metasurface structure made of an epitaxially grown quantum material to deliver a 10x plasmonic electric field enhancement at the desired infrared frequency. I then collaborated with material scientists to realize the structure, and performed advanced nonlinear optical characterization to validate and investigate results.
- **Result:** Our experiments demonstrated a 100x increase in second harmonic generation that was aided by design and choice of the quantum material.
