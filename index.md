I am a Physics PhD researcher at UC San Diego specializing in the design and experimental validation of advanced optical and electromagnetic systems. My work integrates metasurface photonics, nonlinear light–matter interactions, and predictive electromagnetic simulation to translate theory into functional hardware. I build end-to-end platforms—from modeling and device design to measurement and analysis.

## Keystone project
<u>Tunable nonlinear light generation with a quantum material metasurface</u><br>
Developed a metasurface-based photonic device integrated with a quantum material to dramatically enhance nonlinear optical interactions in the infrared. Developed an advanced optical measurement system to demonstrate a 100× increase in nonlinear light generation.
